# CampusFit

## Sales & Strategy Project

### Premium Presence, Zero Permanent Cost

CampusFit is a professional clothing rental service designed for college students who need formal outfits for interviews, internships, presentations and campus events.

## Problem

Students often face:
- High cost of professional clothing
- Limited usage of formal outfits
- Budget constraints during placement season

Professional suits can cost ₹8,000–₹15,000, while students may only wear them once or twice. CampusFit addresses this problem through an affordable rental model. 

## Target Audience

- Final-year students
- MBA/PGDM students
- Placement candidates
- Students attending formal events and presentations

## Business Model

Students can:
1. Select an outfit
2. Try and fit the outfit
3. Rent it for 1–3 days
4. Use it for interviews or events
5. Return it for cleaning

## Pricing

- Basic Formal: ₹399
- Women's Formal Set: ₹599
- Full Suit Package: ₹699
- Premium Executive Suit: ₹999

## Competitive Advantage

CampusFit focuses specifically on the college campus market and offers professional clothing at a lower rental price than purchasing formal wear.

## Go-to-Market Strategy

- Placement committee partnerships
- On-campus fitting booths
- Student society collaborations
- Instagram and LinkedIn campaigns
- Student ambassador and referral programs

## Key Takeaway

CampusFit provides an affordable and convenient solution to students who need professional clothing without making a large one-time purchase.
