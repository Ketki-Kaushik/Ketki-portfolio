# Britannia

## Marketing Hackathon – Advanced Marketing Management

A marketing research project examining why health-conscious consumers are moving away from Britannia and identifying strategies to regain this consumer segment.

## Project Focus

- Understanding changing snacking habits
- Studying health-conscious consumers
- Competitive analysis
- Field research and shelf visibility
- Identifying opportunities for Britannia NutriChoice
- Developing marketing recommendations

## Key Findings

The study found that Britannia has strong consumer trust, wide reach and affordable pricing, but its NutriChoice range has relatively low visibility in healthy snack aisles.

Competitors such as Unibic and Yoga Bar showed stronger visibility and a wider presence in the healthy-snacking space.

## Recommendations

- Improve NutriChoice visibility in healthy snack aisles
- Introduce clearer low-sugar, high-fibre or millet-based products
- Refresh packaging with stronger health communication
- Use influencers and digital promotions to communicate nutritional benefits
