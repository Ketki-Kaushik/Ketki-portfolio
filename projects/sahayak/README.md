# SAHAYAK+

## Digital Marketing Capstone

### Digital Care. Human Connection.

**A digital-first elder-care service designed to help working professionals care for their parents when they cannot be physically present.**

---

## About the Project

I developed a digital marketing strategy for SAHAYAK+, a mobile app and WhatsApp support service for elder care.

The concept focuses on helping working professionals stay connected with and support their parents remotely.

The key communication themes were:

- Daily care
- Safety
- Peace of mind
- Family connection
- Digital convenience

---

## Understanding the Customer

I developed customer personas to understand the needs, concerns and behaviour of the target audience.

### Primary Customer

Working professionals who live away from their parents and want to make sure they are safe and cared for.

### Parent Perspective

The service focuses on making everyday care simpler while helping parents feel supported and connected.

---

## Competitive Analysis

I compared SAHAYAK+ with established elder-care services such as Portea Medical and Apollo HomeCare.

### Portea Medical

**Focus:** Nursing and medical home visits

**Gap:** Service-heavy approach with relatively limited emotional communication.

### Apollo HomeCare

**Focus:** Clinical elder care

**Gap:** More hospital-centric and potentially expensive positioning.

### SAHAYAK+

**Positioning:** Digital-first elder-care service

**Focus:** Daily care and family reassurance

**Opportunity:** Simple, non-clinical and emotion-led communication.

This helped me identify an opportunity for SAHAYAK+ to differentiate through **simplicity, emotional connection and digital convenience**. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Marketing Funnel Strategy

I used the **TOFU–MOFU–BOFU** framework to structure the customer journey.

### TOFU — Awareness

The objective was to attract potential users through:

- Social media
- Blogs
- Educational content
- Infographics

### MOFU — Consideration

The objective was to explain SAHAYAK+'s services and build trust through:

- Testimonials
- Case studies
- Educational videos
- Service explanations

### BOFU — Conversion

The objective was to convert interested users through:

- Clear call-to-actions
- Service booking
- Sign-up opportunities
- Consultation offers

The funnel was designed to move users from **awareness → consideration → conversion**. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Key Learning

This project helped me understand how digital marketing can combine **technology, customer insights and emotional storytelling** to solve a real consumer problem.

My biggest learning was that an elder-care brand should not communicate only its features.

It should communicate the feeling behind the service:

### Peace of mind for children.
### Care and safety for parents.
