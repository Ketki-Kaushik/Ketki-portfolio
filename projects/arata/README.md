# ARATA

## Brand Analysis & Campaign Proposal

### The Hair Behind the Look

**Because every festive look starts with great hair.**

---

## About the Project

I analysed Arata's current brand play and explored how the brand could strengthen its communication through science, education, trends and engaging content.

The project focused on understanding Arata's positioning, competitive landscape and opportunities for a stronger festive campaign.

---

## Decoding Arata's Current Play

### Science-First

- Science and ingredients
- Clinical efficacy

### Product Education

- Hair concerns
- How-to and solutions

### Trend-Led

- Humour and trends
- Cultural moments

### Product Diversity

- Hair, skin and body
- Multiple hair concerns

---

## Competitive Landscape

I studied the positioning, storytelling and content approaches of Arata alongside competitors such as Bare Anatomy and Fix My Curls.

### Arata

**Positioning:** Science-backed haircare for Indian hair and climate

**Storytelling:** Founder-led + science

**Content:** Education + products + trends

### Bare Anatomy

**Positioning:** Personalised haircare for individual needs

**Storytelling:** "Made for your hair"

**Content:** Education + personalisation

### Fix My Curls

**Positioning:** Specialist for curly, oily and wavy hair

**Storytelling:** Curl journey + community

**Content:** Education + routines + community

## Campaign Concept

### ARATA × DIWALI

### THE HAIR BEHIND THE LOOK

I developed a Diwali campaign around the idea that great hair is an important part of every festive look.

The campaign connects haircare with festive styling and explores different looks including:

- Glam
- Sleek
- Curls
- Traditional

The campaign focuses on creating hair that is:

**Smooth • Shiny • Styled • Frizz-free • Polished • Defined • Bouncy • Humidity-ready • Manageable • Festive-ready**

---

## Campaign Execution

### 01 — Creator GRWM

I proposed creator-led "Get Ready With Me" content where creators take the audience through their festive hair preparation step by step.

**Formats:** Reels + YouTube Shorts

### 02 — Choose Your Look

I proposed interactive Instagram Stories and polls where the audience can choose their preferred Diwali look.

This makes the campaign more participative and gives the audience a role in the content.

---

## The Way Forward for Arata

### 01 — Collaborate With Beauty & Lifestyle Creators

Partner with relevant beauty, fashion and lifestyle creators to make Arata part of authentic festive preparation and styling content.

### 02 — Double Down on Entertainment-Led Content

Build on entertainment and humour-led formats where the brand can be integrated naturally into relatable content.

---

## Key Takeaway

The project helped me understand how a haircare brand can move beyond product communication and create a stronger connection between **haircare, culture, trends and consumer experiences**.
